## What we do

Oracle Digital Assistant (ODA) lets employees and customers get things done through conversation: checking a balance, requesting leave, finding a policy or creating an order. We design and build digital assistants that connect to Fusion and your other systems.

## What we build

- Skills for HR, finance, procurement and customer service
- Flows powered by large language models through OCI Generative AI
- Multi-agent designs: router, supervisor and reflection patterns
- Channels for the web, Microsoft Teams and messaging apps

## Arabic, English and more

We design conversations that work in Arabic, English and the other languages your users speak, so every user is served in the language they prefer.
