## What we do

Oracle Integration (OIC) connects Fusion Cloud ERP with the rest of your systems. We design, build and support integrations that are reliable, secure and easy to monitor.

## Typical integrations

- Bank payment files and statements
- CRM, HR and procurement platforms
- E-invoicing and other government platforms
- Legacy and on-premises systems

## Built to run

Every integration we deliver includes error handling, alerts and documentation, so your team knows when something fails and what to do about it. We also take over and stabilise existing integrations built by others.
