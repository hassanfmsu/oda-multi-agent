## What we do

We implement Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP and help you get more from it after go-live. Our consultants combine knowledge of finance and supply chain processes with the technical skills to configure, extend and report on Fusion.

## Modules

- **Financials:** general ledger, payables, receivables, assets and cash management
- **Procurement:** purchasing, sourcing and supplier management
- **Project Management:** costing, billing and project control
- **Supply Chain Management:** inventory and order management

## Beyond go-live

Oracle updates Fusion every quarter. We test each update against your processes, plan for new features and keep your configuration, integrations and reports working. We also help with local requirements such as VAT and integration with ZATCA's e-invoicing platform (Fatoora), working with your e-invoicing provider.
