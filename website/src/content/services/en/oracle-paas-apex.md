## What we do

Fusion is a SaaS product, so it cannot be customised like an on-premises system. When you need screens, workflows or data that Fusion does not offer, we build them on Oracle PaaS and connect them securely to Fusion.

## Typical solutions

- Self-service portals for suppliers, customers or employees
- Approval workflows and data-entry apps
- Reporting and data hubs on Autonomous Database
- Apps that bring AI features to your Oracle data

## Why PaaS

Extensions built on PaaS keep your Fusion setup standard, so quarterly updates stay simple, while your users still get exactly what they need.
