## What we do

Many organisations have more AI ideas than they can deliver. We help you choose the use cases with real value, check that your data and systems are ready, and plan how to move from proof of concept to production safely.

## How we help

- Workshops to find AI use cases and rank them by value and effort
- Assessment of data quality, security and integration readiness
- Governance: policies, access control, monitoring and human oversight
- A roadmap with clear first steps, costs and measures of success

## Built for local requirements

We take Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and national guidance on AI into account from the start, so your AI programme can grow without surprises.
