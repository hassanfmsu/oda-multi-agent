## What we do

An AI agent does more than answer questions. It decides which tools to use, collects the information it needs and completes a task, such as checking a holiday request against HR policy or preparing a reply to a supplier's email.

## Typical use cases

- Employee requests checked against HR policy, then routed for approval
- Invoices and forms read and entered into Fusion ERP
- Email triage and suggested replies
- Agents that combine several tools to answer one question

## How we build them

We design agents with patterns that keep them predictable: a router that picks the right specialist, a supervisor that plans across several tools, and a reflection step that checks the quality of a result before it reaches the user. Important actions always have a human approval step and a full audit trail.

Agents can be built on the AI platform you prefer: OCI Generative AI, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, AWS or open-source models, and they work in any language your users speak.
