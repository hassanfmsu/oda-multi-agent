## What we do

Your organisation already has the answers: in policies, contracts, manuals and the data in your business systems. We build generative AI assistants that find those answers and explain them clearly, in Arabic or English, with a reference to the source.

## Typical use cases

- HR and policy assistants for employees
- Customer service assistants on your website
- Knowledge search across contracts, procedures and technical manuals
- Assistants that answer questions about data in Fusion ERP

## How we build them

We use retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), so answers are based on your own content, not guesswork. Where data residency matters, we design the solution so your data stays where it needs to be. Every assistant includes access control, usage logs and a clear way to measure answer quality.
